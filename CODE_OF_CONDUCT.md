# Code of Conduct

Loam follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to
follow it: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the
work.

To report behaviour that breaks it, email the maintainer at the address on
their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/ramon427). Reports are kept private.
